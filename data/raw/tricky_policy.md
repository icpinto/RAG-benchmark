# Tricky Operations Policy Addendum

This addendum is intentionally written with near-duplicates, exceptions, tables, dates, and distractor text so benchmark runs can stress retrieval, chunking, reranking, and citation behavior.

## Deprecated refund wording (do not use)

Before March 1, 2024, customers were allowed to request a refund within 30 days. That 30-day wording is archived and must not be used for purchases made on or after March 1, 2024.

## Current refund exceptions

For purchases made on or after March 1, 2024, the standard refund period remains 14 days from purchase. Open-box items still qualify for the 14-day refund window if all original accessories are returned. Final-sale items, gift cards, and downloadable software are not refundable unless required by law.

## Shipping edge cases

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after the order leaves the warehouse, not after the customer places the order. Orders placed after 4:00 p.m. local warehouse time begin processing on the next business day.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days after the order leaves the warehouse. Express shipping does not deliver to PO boxes, APO addresses, FPO addresses, or DPO addresses.

| Destination or condition | Service level | Timing or restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contiguous United States | Standard | 3 to 5 business days after warehouse departure |
| Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories | Standard | 6 to 10 business days after warehouse departure |
| PO boxes | Express | Not supported |
| Weather delay declared by carrier | Any service | Add 2 business days to the quoted delivery estimate |

## Support escalation rules

Email support is available Monday to Friday. Chat support is available every day from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, except company holidays.

A support ticket is escalated to a specialist only after two failed troubleshooting attempts or after 48 hours without a customer-visible update, whichever happens first. The escalation clock pauses while the team waits for information requested from the customer.

## Warranty coverage

The limited warranty lasts 365 days from the original purchase date. It covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, cosmetic scratches, consumable parts, unauthorized repairs, or damage caused by using non-approved chargers.

Replacement items inherit the remainder of the original warranty. If fewer than 90 days remain on the original warranty when the replacement ships, the replacement receives a 90-day minimum warranty from the shipment date.

## Account deletion and data retention

Customers can request account deletion from the privacy settings page or by contacting support. Account deletion is completed within 30 calendar days after identity verification.

Order records, invoices, fraud-prevention logs, tax records, and warranty records may be retained after account deletion when retention is required for legal, accounting, security, or compliance reasons. Marketing preferences are removed within 7 calendar days after account deletion is completed.

## Ambiguous abbreviations

ETA means estimated time of arrival in shipping conversations. ETA does not mean employee travel allowance in this policy. RMA means return merchandise authorization and is required before a customer ships back a warranty replacement.
